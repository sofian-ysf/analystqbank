## 12. Site-Wide Technical Actions

### SEO Fixes
1. **Create and submit a sitemap.xml** to Google Search Console
2. **Implement Schema markup** — FAQ schema on all FAQ sections, Course schema on product pages, Review schema for testimonials, Product schema on pricing
3. **Add breadcrumb navigation** across all pages
4. **Run a full broken link audit** using Screaming Frog or Ahrefs
5. **Add canonical tags** to prevent duplicate content indexing
6. **Implement hreflang tags** if targeting both UK and US audiences
7. **Add a robots.txt** file with proper directives

### Analytics & Tracking Setup
8. **Set up full-funnel event tracking in GA4:**
   - Page view → CTA click → Signup page → Signup complete → First question answered → Mock exam started → Payment initiated → Payment complete
9. **Install heatmaps** (Hotjar or Microsoft Clarity) on homepage, pricing section, and signup page
10. **Implement Facebook Pixel + Google Ads conversion tag** (build retargeting audiences now, even before running ads)
11. **Set up A/B testing** (Google Optimize or VWO) — First tests: homepage headline, pricing page layout, CTA copy
12. **Track blog-to-signup attribution** to identify which content actually drives conversions

### Email Infrastructure
13. **Set up an email marketing platform** (ConvertKit, Mailchimp, or Brevo)
14. **Build these automated sequences:**
    - Free trial onboarding (7 emails over 7 days)
    - Trial expiry / upgrade nudge (3 emails)
    - Lapsed user re-engagement (3 emails at 7, 14, 30 days)
    - Blog subscriber nurture (weekly study tips)
    - Post-purchase onboarding and usage encouragement
15. **Create email capture forms** for: exit-intent popup, blog sidebar, flashcards gate, homepage secondary CTA
